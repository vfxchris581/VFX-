HD - procedural 3D and VFX
==========================

Install: drag HD.app onto the Applications folder in this window.

First launch: HD is not notarized by Apple (that needs a paid Apple Developer ID).
macOS will therefore say it cannot check the app for malicious software.
To open it the first time:

  1. Open Applications, right-click (or Control-click) HD.app and choose Open.
  2. Click Open in the dialog.

On macOS 15 and later, if there is no Open button: try to open HD once, then go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".

After that HD opens normally, and double-clicking .hdg scene files opens them in HD.

Command line: the disk image also contains "hdc", the headless tool for cooking,
rendering and exporting scenes. Copy it somewhere on your PATH, for example:

  sudo cp /Volumes/HD*/hdc /usr/local/bin/

Example scenes are in the "examples" folder and under File > Examples in the app.
